# Crapto Studio brand kit (for making posts)

The full guide is `brand/brand-guide.md` in the studio repo. This is what matters when you write a post.

## Identity

- **Name:** always "Crapto Studio", in Latin letters in both languages. Handle **@craptostudio**. Tagline: "Ideas, compiled." / «أفكارك، جاهزة للتشغيل.»
- **What we do:** Unity games, iOS/Android apps, software (web, desktop, tools), custom AI, interactive content and presentations, upgrades of existing projects, mentoring for programming projects and competitions, custom solutions.
- **Not crypto.** Despite the name, nothing about crypto: no coins, tokens, blockchain, trading, rockets, "to the moon", 🚀🌕💎📈🪙💰⚡, rising-price charts or candlesticks.

## Voice (both languages)

| Trait | Sounds like | Never |
|---|---|---|
| Builder | "Here's what we built and how." | Big promises with no details |
| Playful-smart | "Games people want to replay." | Memes, slang, forced jokes |
| Straight-talking | "Honest answer: you may not need AI for this." | "Our AI-powered solution fits every business!" |
| Reliable | "You see a working build every week." | Vague timelines |

- Short sentences, everyday words, "we" and "you". Say what was built, for whom, and what happens next. One clear call to action.
- Use: build, ship, launch, fix, speed up, improve, plan, clear, simple, honest, step by step, working build, clean handover.
- Avoid: revolutionary, disruptive, game-changer, cutting-edge, next-gen, seamless, synergy, leverage, world-class, the best, #1, guaranteed, 100%, cheap, unlimited.
- Honest claims only: no invented numbers, clients, ratings or testimonials.

## Arabic (Modern Standard Arabic)

- Clear, modern, warm product writing. Not classical or ornate («عملاؤنا الكرام»), no dialect.
- Speak to one reader directly («أخبرنا بفكرتك»، «مشروعك»). Lead with verbs («نبني»، «نصمّم»، «نُطلق»). Avoid translationese («نقوم بعملية بناء…»، «يتم تطوير…»).
- **Write it, don't translate it:**

| English | ✗ Word for word | ✓ Our Arabic |
|---|---|---|
| Games people want to replay. | ألعاب يريد الناس إعادة لعبها. | ألعاب تُلعب مرة بعد مرة. |
| Software built around how you work. | برمجيات مبنية حول طريقة عملك. | برمجيات تُبنى على مقاسك. |
| Apps that earn a spot on the home screen. | تطبيقات تكسب مكاناً على الشاشة الرئيسية. | تطبيقات تبقى على شاشتك. |
| Got an idea? Let’s compile it. | لديك فكرة؟ دعنا نقوم بتجميعها. | لديك فكرة؟ لنبنِها معاً. |

- Arabic punctuation: ، ؟ ؛ « ». Western digits (0–9). Product and tech names stay in Latin letters (iOS، Unity، Flutter).
- Avoid: ثوري، غير مسبوق، نقلة نوعية، أحدث التقنيات، الأفضل، رقم 1، مضمون 100%، الأرخص، عملات رقمية، كريبتو، توكن، تداول.
- Address the reader in the masculine singular when needed, but prefer neutral wording («لديك فكرة؟»، «فكرتك»).
- The DM keyword is **START** in English and **«ابدأ»** in Arabic.

## Text on the slides

- Headline: ≤ 44 characters, 2–3 lines. Mark the key 1–3 words as the accent with `*asterisks*` (once). `\n` forces a line break. Sentence case.
- Tag ≤ 24, sub ≤ 64, card text ≤ 34, step title ≤ 22, step text ≤ 46, statement ≤ 90 characters. No emoji on slides.
- `**bold**` works inside cards, steps and CTA body text.
- Icons are [Lucide](https://lucide.dev/icons) names (use `search_icons`). Avoid rocket, zap, coins, bitcoin, gem, flame, trending-up.

## Themes

The animated hero (slide 1) uses the post's theme; all other slides use the dark theme.

| Theme | Background | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| `dark` | Midnight #0B1628 | The default; orange-gradient accent words |
| `blue` | Cobalt #376BB1 | Bold brand moments; amber underline accents |
| `light` | Mist #D4E5F2 | Calm, friendly; cobalt accent words |

Alternate themes with the most recent posts so the grid stays varied.

## Captions

- First line is the hook (≤ 125 characters; it's all people see before "more").
- 2–4 short paragraphs: what it is, who it's for, what we built (and how, in plain words).
- One call to action: `DM us "START"…` / `راسلنا بكلمة «ابدأ»…`
- 3–5 hashtags at the end, always including `#craptostudio`. Arabic captions use Arabic hashtags plus a couple of Latin ones (e.g. `#تطوير_التطبيقات #برمجة #flutter #craptostudio`).
- Alt text ≤ 100 characters per language, describing the cover.
